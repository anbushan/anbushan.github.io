Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026 (version 1.0)

Vettri Thondan (வெற்றித் தொண்டன், "the app", "we") is an Android app developed by Anbushan that shows news, events, announcements, district office-bearers, election candidates and government-scheme information. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: there is no account or sign-in, your settings stay on your phone, and we do not sell or share your personal information.

Unofficial app. Vettri Thondan is an unofficial supporter app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam or the Government of Tamil Nadu.

1. Information stored on your phone

The following is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it, and it is not sent to us.

  • Settings: language, dark theme, which notifications you want, and the district you picked.
  • Notification history: the alerts you received, shown on the app's Notifications screen.
  • Downloaded content: news, events, announcements and articles you opened, saved so the app works offline.
  • Small counters: how many days you opened the app (to decide when to ask for a Play Store rating) and, if you watched a reward video, the time your ad-free period ends.

You can delete downloaded content in More → Clear cached data. Uninstalling the app removes everything above.

2. Approximate location (optional)

During setup the app can ask for your approximate location to pre-select your district. If you allow it, the location is read once on your phone, matched to a district, and then discarded. Only the district name is saved, on your phone. The location is never sent to us. You can skip this and pick a district yourself.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to receive alerts (daily digest, new announcements, new events, live streams and supporter news). To do this, your phone is subscribed to these topics; Firebase assigns your installation a random identifier. We do not receive your name or phone number. You can turn each type of alert off in More → Notifications.

4. Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob (banner, native, interstitial and optional reward-video ads). AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about ad interactions to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA or the UK), you are asked for consent first and can change it any time in More → Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

Reward videos: if you choose to watch a reward video in More → Remove ads for 24 hours, ads are switched off on your phone for 24 hours. No payment is involved.

5. Crash reports and usage statistics

The app may use Google Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase to collect crash logs and general usage events (for example which screens are opened) together with device information. This helps us fix problems. It does not include your name, contacts or messages.

6. Content and links

  • The app downloads its content from our own feed hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, GitHub can see your IP address when the app downloads it.
  • Some buttons open other websites or apps (for example a source website, YouTube, maps or your calendar). Those services have their own privacy policies.
  • Sharing: when you use "Share" or "Share to status", the app creates the image on your phone and opens Android's share menu. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app to share with.
  • Ratings: the app may show Google Play's in-app review prompt; your rating goes to Google Play, not to us.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not ask for or collect your name, phone number, email or contacts.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not track your precise location.
