Vettri Thondan

வெற்றித் தொண்டன் · News, events and schemes in one place

A free Android app in Tamil and English with:

  • Latest news, events and announcements
  • District office-bearers and election candidates
  • Government schemes with a simple "find schemes for me" finder
  • Daily promise card, share-to-status images, home-screen widget and shortcuts
  • Alerts for new announcements, events and live streams

Unofficial supporter app. Not affiliated with Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam or the Government of Tamil Nadu. Please confirm important information with official sources.
