Grabit support

Report a problem

Email anbushanthi001@gmail.com with:

  • the address of the page where it went wrong (if you can share it),
  • what you expected and what happened instead,
  • your Grabit version (chrome://extensions) and Chrome version.

For a video that is not found or will not download: hover the video, open the menu next to the download icon and choose Copy debug info, then paste it into your email. It lists what Grabit saw on the page, not your browsing or your downloads.

Common questions

Nothing is listed for a video. Start playing the video, then open Grabit again. Some sites load the video only when it plays. On feeds, scroll to the post first.

"Protected content (DRM)". The site encrypts this video (as Netflix, Prime Video, Spotify and some Vimeo videos do). Grabit does not download protected content.

YouTube. Grabit does not work on YouTube.

A download failed after a while. Some sites give download links that expire. Reload the page and download again.

Keyboard shortcuts. Settings → Keyboard shortcuts shows them; change them at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
