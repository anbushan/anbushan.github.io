Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Grabit ("the extension", "we") is a Chrome browser extension that finds media and files on the web pages you visit and saves them to your computer. It is developed by Anbushan. In short: Grabit has no account or sign-in, no server and no analytics. What it learns is stored in your browser on your computer. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data. A few optional features contact other services only when you turn them on, as described in section 4.

1. Information stored in your browser

  • Media found on your tabs. To list what can be downloaded, Grabit reads the pages you open and notices the media and file requests they make (addresses, titles, sizes, qualities and thumbnails). This list is kept in the browser's temporary session storage for each tab and cleared when the tab closes or Chrome quits.
  • Downloads. Your download queue, scheduled downloads, download history and favorites are stored in the extension's own storage in Chrome.
  • Partly downloaded files. While a download is running, its pieces are kept in the extension's private storage on your computer so it can be paused and resumed. They are deleted when the file is finished or the download is removed.
  • Settings. Your preferences (file names, folders, presets, theme, language, hidden hosts and similar) are stored in the extension's storage.
  • Saved files. Files you download are saved by Chrome to your downloads folder, where you control them.

None of this is sent to us.

2. How Grabit reads web pages

Grabit asks for access to all websites so it can find media on whatever page you open. It only reads what the page already loaded or shows you, and only to list and download media for you. It does not record your browsing history, read form fields or passwords, change page content (other than adding its own download buttons), or show ads.

Downloads are made directly from the website to your computer using your own browser session, so Grabit can only reach what you can already see. It does not download protected (DRM) content, media from chats where saving has been turned off, or anything from YouTube.

3. Remote code

All of Grabit's code, including the ffmpeg media converter (WebAssembly), is included in the extension package. It does not load or run code from the internet.

5. Permissions

  • Access to all websites – find media and file links on the page you open and download them when you ask.
  • Downloads – save files to your computer, pause, resume and show them in their folder.
  • Side panel – Grabit's interface.
  • Storage and unlimited storage – keep settings, queue and history, and hold partly downloaded files so large downloads can resume.
  • Web requests (read-only) – notice the media files and stream playlists a page loads, to list them.
  • Declarative net request – send the Referer header some video servers require, only on requests Grabit itself makes.
  • Offscreen documents – run the download engine and ffmpeg in the background.
  • Tab capture – record the current tab, only when you press "Record tab".
  • Active tab, context menus and alarms – the toolbar button, "Download with Grabit" on right-click, and scheduled downloads.
  • Identity (optional) – sign in to Google Drive or Dropbox, requested only when you turn on cloud save.
  • Proxy (optional) – requested only when you set up a proxy.

6. Your choices and deleting data

  • Remove items from your history, queue and favorites in the extension, and turn each optional feature off in Settings.
  • Disconnect Google Drive or Dropbox in Settings. You can also remove Grabit's access from your Google or Dropbox account settings.
  • Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything it stored in your browser. Files you downloaded stay in your downloads folder.

7. Children

Grabit is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

Grabit's use of information follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the extension's single purpose, is not sold, is not used for advertising, and is not used to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the extension changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
