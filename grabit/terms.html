1. What the extension does

Grabit lists the video, audio, images and files on the web pages you visit and saves the ones you choose to your computer. It can pick a stream quality, join separate video and audio tracks, convert, trim and extract audio, bundle files as a ZIP, save subtitles, record live streams or the current tab, and pause, resume and schedule downloads. Optionally it can also save files to your Google Drive or Dropbox.

Download links from some sites expire, so a scheduled or resumed download may need the page reloaded. Conversion and joining run on your computer and can be slow or skipped for very large files. Quality labels, sizes and file names come from the website and can be wrong or missing.
