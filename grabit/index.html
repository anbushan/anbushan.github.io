Grabit

A Chrome extension that opens as a side panel and lists every video, audio track, image and file on the page you are on. Pick a quality for each video (HLS and DASH streams included), save straight from the page with a download icon on videos and a "Save" button on posts, save full-size images or bundle them as a ZIP, convert to MP4, MKV, WebM, MP3 or M4A, trim, record live streams, and pause, resume or schedule downloads. Everything runs in your browser — no account, no server.

Grabit does not download from YouTube or protected (DRM) content.
