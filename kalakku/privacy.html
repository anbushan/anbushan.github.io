Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Kalakku ("the app", "we") is an Android video and photo editor developed by Anbushan. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your videos, photos, music and voice recordings are edited on your phone and stay there. We do not run servers that receive your files, and the app has no accounts or sign-in.

1. Your media and projects

  • Photos and videos. With your permission the app reads the photos and videos on your phone so you can pick clips to edit. On Android 14 and above you can allow only selected items. Clips are read where they are; they are not uploaded.
  • Editing happens on your phone. Trimming, effects, filters, text, stickers, music, voice effects, captions, background removal, auto reframe and exporting all run on the device.
  • Drafts. Your projects are saved inside the app so you can continue later. Pictures the app makes for a project (cut-outs, stickers you make, saved frames, thumbnails) and fonts or logos you import are copied into the app's private storage. Other apps cannot read them, and uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • Saving. Finished videos are saved to Movies/Kalakku and pictures to Pictures/Kalakku, where your gallery can see them.
  • Voiceovers. If you record a voiceover, the app uses the microphone only while you are recording. The recording is stored with your project on your phone.
  • Sharing. When you tap Share or "Post to", the video is handed to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp, Instagram or YouTube). From then on, that app's own privacy policy applies.

2. On-device AI features

  • Auto captions and Remove fillers use speech recognition (whisper.cpp) that runs on your phone. The first time you use them, the app downloads the speech model (about 75–140 MB) from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). That download is an ordinary file request, so Hugging Face can see your IP address, as with any website; your audio is never sent.
  • Background removal, the sticker maker, body and face effects and auto reframe use Google ML Kit on the device. Google Play services may download the ML Kit models and may collect anonymous diagnostics about them; your pictures are not sent. See the ML Kit terms.
  • Text to speech uses your phone's text-to-speech engine. The text you type is handled by that engine as described by its provider.

4. Permissions

  • Photos and videos – to pick clips and photos to edit, and (Android 9 and below) to save into the gallery.
  • Microphone (optional) – only to record voiceovers when you tap Record.
  • Notifications (Android 13+, optional) – export progress and the optional daily reminder to finish your drafts.
  • Foreground service – so a long export can finish while the app is in the background; you see a notification while it runs.
  • Internet and network state – for ads, analytics, crash reports, app updates and downloading the speech and ML Kit models.

5. Your choices and deleting data

  • Remove photo, microphone or notification access at any time in Android settings. The daily reminder can be turned off in Kalakku's Settings.
  • Delete a draft from the Home screen (⋮ → Delete). Downloaded speech models stay in the app's storage until you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored privately (drafts, made stickers, imported fonts and logo, speech models, settings). Videos and photos you saved to your gallery stay until you delete them.
  • To ask us to delete analytics or crash data linked to your app-instance ID, email us at the address below.
