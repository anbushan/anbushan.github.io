1. What the app does

The app shows the Tamil calendar and panchangam (thithi, nakshatram, yogam, karanam, nalla neram, Rahu kalam and other timings), festivals, viratham and muhurtham days, rasipalan and peyarchi palan, and lets you make a jathagam, check marriage porutham, set reminders and read Thirukkural, stories and other content.

2. Calculations and accuracy

Timings are calculated on your phone using the Thirukanitham method with Lahiri ayanamsa for the city you choose. Different panchangams, temples and astrologers use different methods and traditions, so dates and times may differ from your local almanac by a few minutes, and some festival dates (for example Islamic festivals, which depend on moon sighting) are approximate. Some tables (such as nalla neram, Gowri panchangam, soolam and karinal) follow common published tables. Please confirm dates and times for important events with your temple, priest or astrologer.

3. Astrology, palan and folk beliefs

They are not professional, medical, legal, financial or marriage advice, and you should not rely on them alone for important decisions. Folk-belief sections are labelled as such.
