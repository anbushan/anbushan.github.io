Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Tamil Calendar (தமிழ் நாட்காட்டி, "the app", "we") is an Android calendar, panchangam and astrology app developed by Anbushan. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app has no accounts or sign-in, all calculations run on your phone, and what you enter (reminders, notes, birth details) is stored only on your phone. We do not run servers that receive your data.

1. Information you enter

  • Reminders and notes. Birthdays, anniversaries, remembrance days, other reminders and notes on a day are saved in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Jathagam and porutham. To make a horoscope or match horoscopes you enter a name, gender, date, time and place of birth. These are used to calculate the chart on your phone. If you save a chart, the details are stored on your phone only. Your birth star for Thara palan is stored the same way.
  • Settings. Your city, language (Tamil, Tanglish or English), theme, text size, god image, notification times, favourite kurals and reading progress are stored on your phone.
  • Backup. "Export (JSON)" creates a backup file of your reminders, notes and saved charts and hands it to the app you choose (for example Drive, email or WhatsApp). Where it goes from there is up to you, and that app's own privacy policy applies. "Import" reads a backup file you pick.
  • Sharing and PDFs. Share images and jathagam / porutham PDFs are made on your phone and handed to the app you choose.

2. Location

Location is optional. If you tap "Detect my location (GPS)", the app asks for approximate (coarse) location once and uses it on your phone to pick the nearest city in its list, so that sunrise, Rahu kalam and nalla neram are correct for you. The coordinates are not stored and are not sent anywhere. The app does not use precise location or track you in the background. You can instead choose your city from the list.

3. Notifications

The daily panchangam notification, festival-eve alerts and your reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. No push server is involved. The app keeps a list of the notifications from the last 30 days on your phone for the "Past notifications" screen; you can clear it there.

4. Content downloaded by the app

Rasipalan and peyarchi / new-year palan text is downloaded from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) and cached for offline use. These are ordinary file requests: nothing about you is sent, but GitHub can see your IP address as with any website. See the GitHub privacy statement. Everything else — panchangam, festivals, jathagam, Thirukkural, stories and sounds — is built into the app and works offline.

5. Information collected by third-party services

The app uses the following Google services. They may collect information as described below and in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

ServiceWhat it may collectWhy
Google AdMobAdvertising ID, IP address, device information, ad interactionsTo show banner ads and to measure them and prevent fraud. The app requests non-personalised ads only, so ads are not chosen from your interests or history.

The app does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising profiles or any other tracking. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android settings → Privacy → Ads.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone. Nothing you enter in the app is sent to us.

6. Permissions

  • Approximate location (optional) – only when you tap "Detect my location (GPS)", to pick your city.
  • Notifications (Android 13+, optional) – the daily panchangam, festival-eve alerts and your reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders – so reminders arrive at the time you set.
  • Internet and network state – for ads and downloading rasipalan text.

The app does not request access to your contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone. "Import" uses the system file picker, which only gives the app the one file you choose.

7. Your choices and deleting data

  • Remove location or notification access at any time in Android settings. Daily notifications and festival-eve alerts can be turned off in the app's Settings.
  • Delete a reminder, note or saved chart inside the app.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it stored. Backup files you exported stay wherever you saved them.
  • Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
