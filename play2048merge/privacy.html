Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

2048 Merge: Slide & Drop ("the app", "we") is an Android game developed by Anbushan. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play. In short: your game progress is stored on your phone, the app has no accounts of its own, and we do not run servers that receive your data. The app is free and supported by ads, with one optional purchase that removes them.

1. Game data on your phone

  • Progress. Scores, coins, power-ups, level stars, trophies, your Versus rating, your theme and sound settings are saved inside the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.
  • Versus. Versus matches are played against a computer opponent on your phone. No match data is sent to us.
  • Sharing and challenge links. When you tap "Share score", the app opens Android's share menu with a short message containing your score, best tile and a link. The link contains only the board number (seed), board size and your score – no name or other personal information. You choose where to send it. Anyone who opens the link sees that score.

2. Information collected by third-party services

The app uses the following Google services. They may collect information as described below and in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Events never contain your name, contacts or files. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics identifiers are only used after you give consent in the privacy message. Your profile and scores are handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and may be visible to other players according to your Play Games settings.

Information collected by these services is encrypted in transit. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android settings → Privacy → Ads.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone other than the services listed above.

3. Permissions

  • Internet and network state – to load ads, send analytics and, if you use it, Google Play Games.
  • Vibration – for haptic feedback when tiles merge. You can turn it off in Settings.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

4. Your choices and deleting data

  • Change your ad consent in the app: Settings → Privacy settings (shown where consent applies).
  • Rewarded ads are always optional – you only see one when you tap a "Watch ad" button.
  • Sign out of Google Play Games, or delete your Play Games profile and its data, in the Google Play Games app or at myaccount.google.com.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone. A cloud save in Google Play Games stays until you delete your Play Games data.
  • To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your app-instance ID, email us at the address below.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
