Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Orbit Pointer ("the app", "we") is an Android app for aligning satellite TV dishes and tracking satellites, developed by Anbushan. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app has no accounts or sign-in, all angle, orbit and sun calculations run on your phone, and what you enter (installation jobs, photos, transponder lists, dishes) is stored only on your phone. We do not run servers that receive your data. A few features fetch public data from outside services, and the app uses Google AdMob for ads, as described in section 4.

1. Information you enter

  • Installation jobs. Customer name and address, equipment, signal readings, notes, quote and invoice items, and follow-up dates that you type in are saved in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Photos. Photos you take with the camera or pick from your gallery for a job are copied into the app's private storage on your phone (reduced in size). They are deleted with the job.
  • Installer details. Your name, company, phone and licence number, used only to print on your own PDF reports, quotes and invoices, are stored on your phone.
  • Lists and settings. Saved dishes, custom satellites, LNB types, transponder lists (including files you import), tracked satellites, "my dish", alert choices and other preferences are stored on your phone.
  • Sharing and exports. PDF reports, quotes, invoices, CSV and Excel files and shared text are made on your phone and handed to the app you choose (email, messaging, drive). From there that app's own privacy policy applies. Shared reports include a link to the app on Google Play.

2. Location

Location is used to calculate where to point a dish and when satellites pass over you. If you allow it, the app reads your position on your phone; you can instead type coordinates. The app does not track you and does not request location in the background. When you save a "my dish" location for daily alerts, those coordinates are stored on your phone and used by the alert check, without reading your current location again.

Your coordinates leave the phone only in these cases:

  • Weather (Open-Meteo). For rain alerts and cloud cover on satellite passes, the app sends the coordinates of your dish or your location to Open-Meteo, a free weather service, which returns a forecast. No identifier is sent with them.
  • Maps (OpenFreeMap). When you open a map screen, map tiles for the area you view are downloaded from OpenFreeMap. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

3. Camera, Bluetooth and local Wi-Fi

  • Camera is used for the AR view, for job photos and for the TV link. Camera images in the AR view are not recorded or uploaded.
  • TV link. When you turn it on, the phone pointed at your TV streams its camera picture over your local Wi-Fi network to the phone at the dish (or a browser on the same network). The stream does not go over the internet and stops when you leave the screen.
  • Bluetooth is used only to connect to a signal meter you choose. Readings stay on your phone. The app does not use Bluetooth to find your location.

5. Permissions

  • Location (approximate or precise) – dish angles, satellite passes and the GPS compass. Optional: you can type coordinates instead.
  • Camera – AR view, job photos and TV link. Optional.
  • Notifications (Android 13+) – daily tip, rain, sun-outage and satellite-pass alerts and job follow-ups. Optional.
  • Bluetooth (nearby devices) – connecting to a signal meter. Optional.
  • Vibration – alignment feedback.
  • Internet and advertising ID – ads, weather, maps, orbit data and community reports. Dish angles and the compass work without internet.

Choosing gallery photos and import files uses the system picker, which gives the app only the items you pick. The app does not request access to your contacts, microphone or call log.

6. Your choices and deleting data

  • Remove location, camera, Bluetooth or notification access at any time in Android settings. Each alert can be turned off in the app.
  • Delete a job (with its photos and invoice items), dish, transponder or satellite inside the app.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it stored on your phone. Files you shared stay wherever you sent them.
  • To remove a community report you published, contact us with the satellite and frequency.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
