1. What the app does

The app calculates azimuth, elevation and LNB skew for geostationary satellites and guides dish alignment with a compass, sun compass, GPS compass, AR camera view, 3D dish model, bubble level, inclinometer and elevation meter. It also offers TV-provider presets, a map, rain and sun-outage alerts, satellite tracking and pass predictions (ISS and others), LNB, cable, dB, antenna and Unicable calculators, transponder lists, installer jobs with photos, PDF reports, quotes and invoices, a Bluetooth signal-meter connection and a local Wi-Fi TV link.

2. Accuracy

Angles are calculated on your phone from your location and the satellite's orbital position. Satellite positions come from public orbital data and are estimates; operators move satellites, and some satellites drift ("inclined orbit"). TV-provider presets were checked against public sources but can change. Phone compasses are affected by metal, magnets and calibration, and GPS and sensors vary between phones. Weather, sun-outage and pass predictions are forecasts. LNB, cable, antenna and Unicable figures are typical values; the labels on your equipment are the authority. Always confirm the final dish position with a signal meter or your receiver's signal screen.

3. Safety

Installing a dish often means working at height and near electrical cables. Use proper equipment and follow local safety rules, or use a qualified installer. Never look directly at the sun, including when using the sun compass. Do not use the app while driving.
