Vannam Call Screen

A clean, iPhone-style phone app you'll enjoy using. Give every person a full-screen Contact Poster, choose from 60+ call themes (Live, Emoji, Glass, Neon and Indian festivals) or use your own photo or video, and pick original ringtones or make one from your music. Recents with Missed, Voicemail and Unknown Callers, Favourites, contact lists, speed dial, Remind Me, call waiting and conference calls. Silence unknown callers, block numbers, hidden callers, number series or whole countries. Emergency SOS with Medical ID. No account — your contacts and call history stay on your phone.
