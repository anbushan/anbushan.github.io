The app works as your phone app: it places and answers calls, shows Recents, voicemail, Favourites and Contacts, and lets you create and edit contacts. 2. Default phone app

Android only lets the default phone app show call screens, ring and block calls. While Vannam is your default phone app it handles all your calls. You can switch back to another phone app at any time in Android settings.

3. Emergency calls

Do not rely on the app as your only way to get help, and keep your Medical ID up to date — it is information you enter yourself and is not checked by us.
