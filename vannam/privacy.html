Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Vannam Call Screen ("the app", "we") is an Android phone app (dialer) with customisable call screens, developed by Anbushan. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app has no accounts or sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. Your contacts, call history and everything you create in the app stay on your phone. The app does not send any of your information to us or to anyone else, and we do not run servers that receive it.

1. Information the app uses on your phone

  • Contacts. To show who is calling, list and search your contacts, and let you create and edit them (name, phone numbers, email, company, website, address, birthday, notes, pronunciation, photo, ringtone). Changes are saved to your phone's contacts, like any phone app.
  • Call history and voicemail. To show Recents, missed calls, call statistics per contact and visual voicemail (where your carrier supports it). You can delete calls or export your call history to a file you choose.
  • Calls. As your default phone app, Vannam places, answers, holds, merges and ends calls through Android. It cannot record calls or hear call audio.
  • Things you create in the app. Call themes, Contact Posters, contact lists, hidden contacts, call notes, quick replies, speed dial, blocking rules, reminders, ringtones you make, Medical ID and emergency contacts, and your settings. These are stored only in the app's storage on your phone. Photos and sounds you pick are copied into the app's storage.
  • Number location. For unknown callers the app shows the region and carrier ("Chennai · Airtel") using data built into the app. No internet lookup is made.
  • Blocked numbers. Stored in Android's own blocked-numbers list, so other phone apps respect them too.

2. Optional features

  • Notification access (Flash for notifications and Edge lighting) – the app only notices that a notification arrived, to blink the flash or light the screen edge. It does not read, store or send notification content. Off by default.
  • Display over other apps (Edge lighting) – used only to draw the glowing screen edge. Off by default.
  • Fingerprint / screen lock (Hidden contacts) – checked by Android; the app never sees your fingerprint or PIN.
  • Announce caller name – uses your phone's text-to-speech engine.
  • Backup – if Android backup is turned on, your Vannam settings and themes may be included in your Google account backup, under Google's control.
  • Sharing – contact cards (vCard, QR code), exported contacts and call history files go only where you choose; from there that app's privacy policy applies.

3. Emergency calls

Emergency SOS calls the emergency number (112) or the emergency contacts you choose. Your Medical ID is shown only on your phone when you open it; it is not sent anywhere.

4. Information sent off your phone

None. The current version contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or push-notification services, and does not use the advertising ID. The daily "theme of the day" notification is created on your phone. Links you tap (such as a contact's website, email or WhatsApp) open in the app you choose, under that app's privacy policy. We do not sell your information. If a future version adds any service that sends data off your phone, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

5. Permissions

  • Contacts – show callers and manage contacts.
  • Phone and call log – work as your phone app: place and answer calls, show Recents and voicemail. Granted when you set Vannam as your default phone app.
  • Notifications and full-screen notifications – show incoming calls, missed calls, reminders and the optional daily theme.
  • Foreground service (phone call) – keep an ongoing call running correctly.
  • Vibrate, wake lock – ring and vibrate for calls.
  • Display over other apps – optional Edge lighting.
  • Internet – declared by a library included in the app; the current version does not send your information anywhere (see section 4).

Camera flash for Flash alerts uses torch mode and needs no camera permission. The app does not access your location, microphone (outside calls handled by Android) or camera.

6. Your choices and deleting data

  • Remove any permission in Android settings, or stop using Vannam as your default phone app at any time.
  • Turn off the daily notification, Flash and Edge lighting in Settings, and use Settings → Reset settings.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything Vannam stored. Your phone's contacts and call history remain, as they belong to Android.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
