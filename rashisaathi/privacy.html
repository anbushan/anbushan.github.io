Rashi Saathi (राशि साथी, "the app", "we") is an Android astrology and Panchang app developed by Anbushan. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: there is no account or sign-in, and your birth details and saved kundlis are stored only on your phone. Rashifal, Panchang and kundli calculations run on your phone. We do not run servers that receive your data, and we do not sell it. The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, which receives some device information as described in section 4.

1. Information stored on your phone

The following is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it.

  • Settings. Language, your rashi (zodiac sign), your city, theme and text size, chart style and ayanamsa, notification choices and times.
  • Saved kundlis (birth profiles). For each person you add: name, gender, date, time and city of birth, and an optional anniversary date. Used only to calculate their kundli, matching, birthday reminders and personal daily insights.
  • Usage within the app. Your daily-open streak, mala (japa) count, festival reminders you turned on, and whether the app has already asked you for a rating.

3. When information leaves your phone

Only in these cases, and only with the information described:

  • Sharing. Share cards, greeting images, the Panchang image and the kundli PDF are made on your phone and handed to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp). The kundli PDF contains the person's name and birth details; share it only with people you trust.
  • Backup file. "Back up saved kundlis" writes your saved profiles to a file in a location you choose. Anyone with that file can read it.
  • Android backup. If Google backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the app's settings and saved kundlis in your phone's encrypted backup to your Google account, so they come back when you set up a new phone. This is managed by Android and Google, not by us.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows banner, native and full-screen ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), device and app information, and how you interact with ads, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

  • Your birth details, saved kundlis and the content you view in the app are not given to the ad SDK by us.
  • Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice later.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).

Other services: occasionally (after a 7-day streak) the app may invite you to rate it using Google Play In-App Review, which is handled by Google Play under your Google account; we do not see whether or what you rated. Read-aloud uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine. The app does not include analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

5. Your choices and deleting data

  • Turn off any notification type in the app's Settings, and any permission in Android settings.
  • Delete saved kundlis inside the app (Kundli → saved list → delete).
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it stored. Backup files you exported stay wherever you saved them.
  • Because we hold no data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. For data held by Google for advertising, use Google's controls linked in section 4.

6. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Protect your phone with a screen lock, and keep exported backup files and kundli PDFs somewhere safe.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
