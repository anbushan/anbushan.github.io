1. What the app does

The app provides rashifal (horoscopes) for the 12 signs; a daily Panchang (tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, sunrise and sunset, Rahu Kaal, Choghadiya, Hora, muhurats and related timings); a birth chart (kundli) with divisional charts, dashas, Ashtakvarga, strength, yogas, doshas, Lal Kitab, KP and written readings; kundli matching; a muhurat finder; a festival, vrat and eclipse calendar; numerology, name, tarot and other calculators; devotional texts and tools; and share images and reminders.

3. Accuracy of calculations

Panchang timings, planetary positions, kundli charts and muhurats are calculated on your phone with standard astronomical formulas and the ayanamsa you choose. Different panchangs and traditions use different methods, so timings and results can differ from other sources by a few minutes or in interpretation, and depend on the birth time and place you enter. Festival and vrat dates follow common North-Indian practice and may differ from your local or family tradition. Eclipse times and visibility are calculated for your chosen city. Please confirm important muhurats and dates with your family priest or local panchang.
