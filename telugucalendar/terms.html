1. What the app does

The app shows the Telugu calendar and panchangam (tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, Rahu kalam, Yamagandam, Durmuhurtham, Varjyam, hora, Gowri panchangam, Choghadiya and other timings), festivals, holidays, fasting and muhurtham days, eclipses and special yogas, rasi phalalu, and lets you make a jathakam with divisional charts, read horoscope reports, check marriage matching, find muhurthams and baby names, set reminders and notes, and read poems, slokas, stories and traditions.

2. Calculations and accuracy

Timings are calculated on your phone from astronomical positions with the Lahiri ayanamsa, for the city or coordinates you choose, following the Amanta month system used in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana unless you change it. Different panchangams, temples and astrologers use different methods and traditions, so dates and times may differ from your local almanac by a few minutes. Some festival dates (for example Islamic festivals, which depend on moon sighting) and state holidays follow government notifications that can change. Bank holidays shown follow the RBI's fixed rules and state public holidays; confirm with your bank. Please confirm dates and times for important events with your temple, priest or astrologer.

They are not professional, medical, legal, financial or marriage advice, and you should not rely on them alone for important decisions. Health tips in the Arogyam section are general traditional guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor for health matters. Blood donation information is general guidance; follow the blood bank's instructions.
