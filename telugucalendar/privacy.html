Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Telugu Panchangam (తెలుగు పంచాంగం, "the app", "we") is an Android calendar, panchangam and astrology app, with a Wear OS tile, developed by Anbushan. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app has no accounts or sign-in, all calculations run on your phone, and what you enter (birth details, reminders, notes) is stored only on your phone. We do not run servers that receive your data.

1. Information you enter

  • Profile, family and friends. To make a jathakam, a horoscope report, a personal muhurtham or a marriage match you can enter a name, gender, date, time and place of birth for yourself and for people you choose to add. These are used to calculate charts on your phone and are stored on your phone only. Saved matches are stored the same way.
  • Reminders, notes and bookmarks. Reminders (by date or by tithi), notes on calendar days and bookmarked reading items are saved in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Blood donation log. If you record your blood donations, the dates are stored on your phone only, to show when you can donate again.
  • Settings. Your city or coordinates, language, theme, notification time and other preferences are stored on your phone.
  • Backup. "Backup" creates a file with your settings, profile, people, reminders, notes and bookmarks and hands it to the app you choose (for example Drive, email or WhatsApp). Where it goes from there is up to you, and that app's own privacy policy applies. "Restore" reads a backup file you pick.
  • Sharing, PDFs and calendar export. Share images, greeting cards, jathakam, matching, report and calendar PDFs, and .ics calendar files are made on your phone and handed to the app you choose.
  • Feedback. "Feedback" and "Report error" open your email app with the app version, Android version and device model filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send the email.

2. Location

Location is optional. If you tap "Use my current location" during setup, the app asks for approximate (coarse) location and uses it on your phone to pick the nearest city in its list so that sunrise, Rahu kalam and other timings are correct. The location is stored in the app's settings on your phone and is never sent to us or anyone else by the app. The app does not use precise location or track you in the background. You can instead choose a city from the list or type coordinates.

3. Notifications

The daily panchangam notification, festival-eve reminders, the Rahu kalam alert and your own reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. No push server is involved. The app keeps the last 100 notifications on your phone for the "Notifications" history screen; you can clear it there.

4. Information collected by third-party services

The app uses the following Google service. It may collect information as described below and in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

ServiceWhat it may collectWhy
Google AdMobAdvertising ID, IP address, device information, ad interactionsTo show banner, native, full-screen (interstitial) and rewarded ads, to measure them and to prevent fraud. Ads may be personalised where you allow it.

Where the law requires it (for example the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent message before requesting ads, and you can change your choice under Settings → Ad privacy options.

Apart from the data AdMob handles as described above, the app does not add its own analytics, crash reporting or advertising profiles. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android settings → Privacy → Ads.

Some features open another app or website, whose own policy then applies:

  • Temples and blood banks. "Directions" and "Blood banks near me" open Google Maps or your maps app.
  • Live darshan opens official broadcaster pages on YouTube or their website.
  • Blood donation can open the government e-RaktKosh website and your phone dialer for 108 or 104.

We do not sell your information. Nothing you enter in the app is sent to us.

5. Permissions

  • Approximate location (optional) – only when you choose "Use my current location", to pick your city.
  • Notifications (Android 13+, optional) – the daily panchangam, festival reminders, Rahu kalam alert and your reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders – so notifications arrive at the minute you choose.
  • Run at startup – to re-schedule your notifications after the phone restarts.
  • Set wallpaper – only when you tap "Set" on a wallpaper made by the app.
  • Internet, network state and advertising ID – for ads.

The app does not request access to your contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone. "Restore" uses the system file picker, which only gives the app the one file you choose.

6. Your choices and deleting data

  • Remove location or notification access at any time in Android settings. The Rahu kalam alert and personal alerts can also be turned off in the app's Settings.
  • Change your ad consent choice under Settings → Ad privacy options, where shown.
  • Delete a person, reminder, note, saved match, donation record or notification history inside the app. "Reset settings" clears your preferences.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it stored. Backup files you exported stay wherever you saved them.
  • Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
