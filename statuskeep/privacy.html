Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

StatusKeep ("the app", "we") is an Android app developed by Anbushan. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your photos, videos, audio and statuses stay on your phone. We do not run servers that receive your files, and the app has no accounts or sign-in.

1. Files on your phone

  • Status folder. The app reads only the status folder you choose in Android's folder picker, to show statuses you have already viewed in your chat app. You can remove this access at any time in Android settings.
  • Saving. Statuses and edits you save are copied to your phone's public folders (by default Pictures/StatusKeep, Movies/StatusKeep and Music/StatusKeep; you can change the folder in Settings). They are visible to your gallery and music apps.
  • Auto-save (optional). If you turn it on, a background job copies new statuses to the same folders about every 15 minutes. It runs only on your phone.
  • Keep statuses after they disappear (optional). If enabled, the app keeps private copies inside its own storage for the number of days you choose (1, 2 or 7), then deletes them. Other apps cannot read them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • Media cleaner (optional). If you use it, you grant access to your chat app's Media folder so the app can list large files. Files are only deleted after you select them and confirm.
  • Photos, videos and songs you pick. With the Android photo picker or file picker the app only gets the items you choose. Editing, trimming, merging, making videos from photos, changing sound and removing backgrounds all happen on your phone.
  • Background removal uses Google ML Kit, which runs on the device. Google Play services may download the ML Kit model and may collect anonymous diagnostics about it; your photo is not sent. See the ML Kit terms.
  • Sticker packs you make are stored inside the app. When you tap "Add to WhatsApp", only WhatsApp is allowed to read that pack from the app.
  • Shared to StatusKeep. When you share a photo, video, audio or text to the app from another app, it is copied to the app's temporary storage so you can edit it, and cleared later.

3. Permissions

  • Folder access (picked by you) – to show statuses, and for the optional cleaner, to delete the files you choose.
  • Notifications (Android 13+, optional) – download progress, auto-save results, reminders before statuses disappear, festival reminders.
  • Camera (optional) – to take a photo you want to edit. Photos stay on your phone.
  • Fingerprint / face unlock (optional App lock) – handled by Android; the app never receives your biometric data.
  • Modify system settings (optional) – only if you ask the app to set a sound as your ringtone, notification or alarm tone.
  • Storage (Android 9 and below only) – required by those Android versions to save into the gallery.
  • Internet – for ads, analytics, app updates and downloading the background-removal model.

4. Direct chat

The "Direct chat" tool opens your chat app with the number you type. The number is not stored or sent to us; only your country code choice is remembered on your phone.

5. Your choices and deleting data

  • Remove folder access, notifications or camera access at any time in Android settings.
  • Change your ad consent in the app: Settings → Privacy & ad choices (shown where consent applies).
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored privately (settings, kept copies, sticker packs, drafts). Files you saved to your gallery stay until you delete them.
  • To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your app-instance ID, email us at the address below.
