Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Panchanga Plus (ಪಂಚಾಂಗ ಪ್ಲಸ್, "the app", "we") is an Android Kannada calendar, panchanga and astrology app developed by Anbushan. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app has no accounts or sign-in, all panchanga calculations run on your phone, and what you enter (family birth details, reminders, notes) is stored only on your phone. We do not run servers that receive your data. The app uses Google AdMob for ads and Google Firebase to understand app usage and fix crashes, as described in section 4.

1. Information you enter

  • Family profiles. You can save a name, gender, date, time and place of birth for yourself and family members, to reuse in marriage matching, the nakshatra finder and rashi bhavishya. The app calculates the nakshatra, rashi and lagna on your phone and stores the profile on your phone only.
  • Tool inputs. Dates and details you enter in tools such as the nakshatra finder, marriage matching, the Shraddha tithi finder, the age calculator and the date converter are used on your phone to show the result.
  • Reminders, notes and favourites. Reminders (once, every year by date, or every year by tithi), notes on calendar days and favourites are saved in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Settings. Your city or coordinates, language, janma rashi and nakshatra, theme, text size, notification time and other preferences are stored on your phone.
  • Sharing, PDFs and calendar export. Share images, PDF downloads (monthly panchanga, festival list, matching report) and .ics calendar files are made on your phone. They are saved where you choose with the system file picker, or handed to the app you choose to share with. From there that app's own privacy policy applies.

2. Location

Location is optional. If you tap "Allow" during setup, Android asks you to grant location; you can choose approximate or precise. The app reads your current position once, on your phone, so that sunrise, Rahu kala and every other timing is calculated for where you are. The coordinates are stored in the app's settings on your phone and are never sent to us or anyone else by the app. The app does not track you or use location in the background. You can instead pick your city from the list.

3. Notifications and widgets

The daily panchanga notification, the Chandrashtama alert and your own reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. Home-screen widgets (today's panchanga, month calendar, Rahu kala and tithi) are calculated and refreshed on your phone. No push server is involved.

5. Permissions

  • Location (approximate or precise, optional) – only when you choose "Allow" in setup, to calculate timings for where you are.
  • Notifications (Android 13+, optional) – the daily panchanga, Chandrashtama alert and your reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders – so notifications arrive at the minute you choose.
  • Run at startup – to re-schedule your notifications after the phone restarts.
  • Internet, network state and advertising ID – for ads and the Firebase services above. Panchanga calculations work without internet.

The app does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera or microphone. Saving a PDF or calendar file uses the system file picker, which only gives the app the one file you create.

6. Your choices and deleting data

  • Remove location or notification access at any time in Android settings. Daily notifications and the Chandrashtama alert can also be turned off in the app's Settings.
  • Change your ad consent choice under Settings → Ad privacy options, where shown.
  • Delete a family profile, reminder or note inside the app. "Reset all settings" clears your preferences.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it stored on your phone. Files you saved or shared stay wherever you put them.
  • We hold no data about you that could identify you. Analytics and crash data held by Google are deleted under Google's retention periods.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
