1. What the app does

The app shows the Kannada calendar and panchanga (tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, Rahu kala, Yamaganda, Gulika, Durmuhurta, Varjya, Amrita kala, hora, Gowri panchanga, Choghadiya and other timings), festivals, vratas and holidays, Ekadashi parana, muhurta days and rashi bhavishya. It also offers a nakshatra finder, marriage matching, Shraddha tithi finder, baby name letters, family profiles, reminders and notes, PDF and calendar downloads, widgets, and reading on shlokas, vastu and Karnataka traditions.

2. Calculations and accuracy

Timings are calculated on your phone from astronomical positions with the Lahiri ayanamsa, for the city or coordinates you choose, following the Amanta month system used in Karnataka. Different panchangas, mathas, temples and astrologers use different methods and traditions, so dates and times may differ from your local almanac by a few minutes, and some festivals may be observed on a different day. Holidays follow government notifications that can change. Please confirm dates and times for important events with your temple, priest or astrologer.
