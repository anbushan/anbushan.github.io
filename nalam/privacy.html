Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Nalam (நலம், "the app", "we") is an Android health and fitness tracker developed by Anbushan. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app has no accounts or sign-in, and the health information you enter or the app records is stored only on your phone. We do not run servers that receive your data, and we do not sell or share it.

1. Information stored on your phone

The following is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it.

  • Profile and goals. Name, gender, year of birth, height, weight, daily goals and settings.
  • Activity. Steps (per hour and per day), active minutes, floors, workouts, GPS routes and saved routes, personal records.
  • Health entries. Food, water, sleep, weight, body fat, blood pressure, blood glucose, heart rate, heart-rate variability, SpO₂, stress, lab results, mood, mindfulness sessions, habits, fasting, period and symptom data, medicines and doses taken.
  • Family members. Names and the readings and medicines you record for people you look after.
  • Emergency card. Blood group, allergies, conditions and an emergency contact's name and phone number.
  • Snore detection. A summary of each night and short audio clips around snores (see section 2).
  • Step challenge. The names and weekly step totals of family and friends whose challenge messages you add (see section 3).

3. When information leaves your phone

Only in these cases, and only with the information described:

  • Export, backup and PDF report. Saved to a file and location you choose. Where it goes from there is up to you.
  • Sharing. Share cards (for example your steps this week, a workout, a badge or a streak), the weekly report and a family member's summary are made on your phone and handed to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp). The message includes a link to Nalam on Google Play.
  • Step challenge. "Share my steps" puts your name and your step counts for the current week into a message (as a short code) that you send yourself, for example to a WhatsApp group. Anyone who receives that message can read it. When you add a friend, the app reads the code from the text you copied or pasted; it is stored only on your phone.
  • SOS. The SOS text message goes from your phone, through your mobile carrier, to the emergency contact you set. It contains your name (if set), that you need help or that a fall was detected, a Google Maps link to your current location with its accuracy (or "Location unavailable"), and the time. If the app cannot send SMS, it opens your messaging app with the message filled in instead.
  • Health Connect. Handled by Android under the permissions you grant.

5. Health information

Your health information is used only to show it back to you inside the app: charts, goals, reminders, scores and summaries. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone unless you share it yourself as described in section 3.

6. Your choices and deleting data

  • Turn off any permission at any time in Android settings, or under My page → Permissions in the app. Features that need it will stop working until you allow it again.
  • Delete individual entries, family members, saved routes or challenge friends inside the app.
  • Disconnect Health Connect under My page → Connected services & data, or in Android's Health Connect settings.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it stored. Backup files you exported stay wherever you saved them.
  • Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

7. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Protect your phone with a screen lock, and keep exported backup files somewhere safe, because anyone with the file can read it.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Not a medical device

Nalam is a wellness diary. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. See the Terms & Conditions.
