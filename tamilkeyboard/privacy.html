Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Tamil Keyboard ("the app", "we") is an Android keyboard and companion app developed by Anbushan. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: what you type is processed on your phone and is never sent to us or anyone else. We have no servers and no accounts, and the keyboard shows no ads. The only typed text that can leave your phone is a GIF search word you choose to send to GIPHY, and your voice when you use voice typing through your phone's speech service.

1. What you type

  • On your phone. Tanglish-to-Tamil conversion, word suggestions, next-word suggestions, spelling help and English auto-correction, gesture typing and emoji suggestions all run on your phone.
  • Learned words. To improve suggestions, the keyboard remembers words you pick and which words follow each other. This is stored only on your phone. You can see and delete it in the app under "My words", or turn learning off in Typing settings.
  • Private fields. In password fields and fields that apps mark as private (for example incognito browser tabs), the keyboard does not learn words, does not save clipboard history and does not show suggestions.
  • Text shortcuts you create are stored only on your phone.

2. Clipboard history

Text you copy is kept in a clipboard history on your phone (the 40 most recent items, plus any you pin). Items Android marks as sensitive, such as copied passwords, are skipped. You can clear it in Typing settings → Privacy → Clear clipboard history.

3. Voice typing

When you open the 🎤 panel, the keyboard uses your phone's speech recognition service (usually Google's) and the microphone permission. That service may process your voice on its servers, unless you have installed offline speech recognition for Tamil; its provider's privacy policy applies. We do not receive or keep any audio. The microphone is used only while the voice panel is open.

4. Handwriting, translation and photo stickers

Handwriting recognition, Tamil–English translation and background removal for photo stickers use Google ML Kit. The first time you use them, the models (for example the Tamil handwriting model, about 20 MB, and the translation language pack, about 30 MB) are downloaded from Google. After that they run on your phone; your drawings, text and photos are not uploaded. Google Play services may collect anonymous diagnostics about ML Kit. See the ML Kit terms.

5. GIFs

GIFs come from GIPHY. When you open the GIF tab, the topic you pick, or the word you typed if you choose to search with it, is sent to GIPHY to find GIFs, together with standard technical information such as your IP address. Nothing else you type is sent, and the typed word is never offered for GIF search in password or private fields. See GIPHY's privacy policy.

6. Status saver and chat stickers

If you allow it, the app reads the status or sticker folder you choose in Android's folder picker, only to show those files to you. Files you save are copied to your phone's Pictures or Movies folder. Nothing is uploaded. You can remove access at any time in Android settings.

7. Opening apps from the keyboard

To suggest apps while you type and to show the ⊞ Apps panel, the keyboard reads the list of apps on your phone that have a home-screen icon. This list never leaves your phone. You can turn app suggestions off in Typing settings.

9. Permissions

  • Keyboard (input method) – you turn Tamil Keyboard on in Android settings so it can type for you. Android shows a standard warning for every keyboard you install that it could collect what you type; Tamil Keyboard does not send what you type anywhere, except as described in sections 3 and 5.
  • Microphone (optional) – only for voice typing, while the voice panel is open.
  • Folder access (optional) – only the status or sticker folder you choose, for the status saver and chat stickers.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) – to save statuses to your gallery.
  • Vibration – for the optional vibration on key press.
  • Internet – for usage statistics, GIFs, voice typing and downloading the ML Kit models.

10. Your choices and deleting data

  • Delete learned words in "My words", clear clipboard history in Typing settings → Privacy, and turn off learning, app suggestions or usage statistics in Typing settings.
  • Remove microphone or folder access at any time in Android settings.
  • Everything the app stores is on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it all (learned words, clipboard history, shortcuts, stickers you made, settings). Statuses you saved to your gallery stay until you delete them.
  • To ask us to delete usage statistics linked to your app-instance ID, email us at the address below.
